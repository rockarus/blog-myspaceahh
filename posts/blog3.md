---
title: "I don't really like poetry"
date: "2026-08-13"
---

As a little child, I used to write a fair bit of poetry and a ton of prose, usually as short mystery stories. I respect the greats of poetry I grew up with; Rumi, Umar Khayyam, Jorge Luis Borge. But...I'm definitely of the side that prefers prose. Poetry, usually being praised for all its artistic beauty and elegance, is really just meant to be flowery. I say real skill and beauty lies in prose that presents itself seemingly drier.
Put it this way; poetry is like how Indian instant pasta is. It's loaded with masala to give you all kinds of commercially accepted flavor; it's not that easy to make bland instant pasta when you add in the spicemix. But that's the thing; it's meant to be bursting with complex flavor, and if the quality isn't really great you could just end up with an oily goopy mix of too much flavor. Prose is like traditional pasta; all it takes is water and flour really, with some cheese and some tomato-based saucy flavor on top. But that lack of bombardment is really what gives space to let the inherent flavors shine; good prose is about evoking feeling subliminally, while presenting a general emotional tone on the surface. 
It might sound very old-fashioned, but I suppose it really just draws parallels to stability, because sometimes there is beauty in nothingness. Sometimes you just wanna sit down after running around all day. Sometimes you just wanna get up on a Sunday and eat a 5-Star. I guess there is a fine line between being attracted to the absence of something because it's been too long being overstimulated and just because of the inherent nature of the void. But maybe the second option hints at something darker because you'd only choose the void when the void is already within you. As humans, we are hardwired to always push for the next thing for survival, so choosing the void would mean that instinct is suppressed because something is amiss. 
I guess that's just a gentle reminder to stop and think if you or any of your friends are choosing the void yourselves. We are in an IIT after all.
