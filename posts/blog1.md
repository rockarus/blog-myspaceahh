---
title: "Does money erase your soul?"
date: "2026-04-12"
---

Have you ever watched American Psycho? It is a movie targetting the soullessness of a society so driven by materialism that everyone and everything is reduced to bare cogs in a wheel, where nothing can fall out of predetermination. When I, for one, observe people that grew up rich and poor, the first thing that meets the eye is character. It feels so...unnervingly uncanny to observe a person that grew up subscribed to mainstream culture in a materialistic environment, taught to master interpersonal skills and appear diplomatic.
Contrast that to the young man working his way up through the world, bold and unafraid to let his true colours bleed, but always a little rough on the edges when it comes to polished expression. 
You'd never want to oppose the silver spooner, hell how could anyone be on his bad side? But it is heartening to watch the dark horse trot through when he carries uncut originality that shines like a diamond fresh out of the mines. 
It really makes one wonder if, in the end, you do really "sell" your soul literally. Or do you invest your soul into wealth instead? Maybe it's a game of perspectives.
