I don't really know what to write, but I want to write something. There's a bittersweet feeling every time you survive another trip around the sun. I'm not sure how to describe a feeling when there's no rational thought to go along with it. It's just an abstract concept. 

I guess you could write anything to fill the space, be it the joy of having lived vs the helplessness against your impending fate, or whatever really. Guess I'll spare you that ramble, I'm sure you would have felt that enough times throughout your life that it's probably equivalent to asking a person with a broken leg how it broke. However, it is probably good to start with notes that could stir some thought, even if they're terribly cliched like how stevia is terribly sweet (I hate that thing).

There is some inherent mental clarity to gain in communication and we know humans are naturally social animals. Does it mean, then, that introversion is a subconscious response to mild but constant trauma against sociality? In fact, what does it take for one to recognise on one's own if one has even faced trauma? I suppose, to avoid getting bound in a slowly thickening quicksand pool and falling into the dark pit of bias it is imperative to frequently shift around by perspective just so you see the sunlight of knowledge with the clarity of not having any quicksand on you. Only then would you even be able to look back and recognise your past from a different viewpoint, and who knows how that might change your view of the present? Recognising the cause of introversion might just be one case of that. Of course, this could be a very reductive take but maybe it is still accurate enough to correlate well while being crude enough to not overanalyse.

Just a random thought.
